Questions, answered

Chocolate, guests, timing, power, leftovers and the deposit. If your question is not here, call 724-339-9658 — we answer the phone.

Twelve answers

Everything we are asked

Still unsure? Email info@chocolatefountainheaven.com or call 724-339-9658.

01

Can you tell me more about what you do?

Chocolate Fountain Heaven offers the best quality chocolate fountain services in the area. We are insured by The Hartford and have been in business for more than 23 years.

02

What kind of chocolate do you use?

Sephra chocolate, formulated for chocolate fountains so no oil is needed. Milk, dark or white chocolate, in all our fountains. It has had rave reviews.

03

What is the maximum number of guests a fountain will serve?

We have served 1,800 people at festivals.

04

Which dipping items are not recommended?

Anything that breaks apart easily or crumbles — especially pound cake, which compromises the smooth flow of the chocolate. We recommend angel food cake instead: it has a more substantial texture than the regular version.

05

Do you provide the dipping items?

Yes. Because of liability and freshness, only Chocolate Fountain Heaven provides the dipping items. They are priced on a per-person basis and included in your quote.

06

Can the fountain be used outside?

We do not recommend it. The fountain has such a sweet, inviting aroma that every heavenly creature in the garden is tempted to partake. Chocolate also needs a stable temperature, and outdoor conditions affect its integrity.

In the past, we have had success outdoors ONLY when the fountain was set up in the rear of the tent with three sides down, so that no wind hits the fountain. Should you choose this, the contract will state that there is no refund should we have to turn the fountain off for a period of time. It is still dippable from the base, just not from the typical cascade of the chocolate.

07

How long can we have the fountain?

Most people use it during the appetizer segment. Our package includes two and a half hours of fountain enjoyment. If you want it throughout the event, overtime beyond the contract is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant.

08

Who is responsible for set-up, tear-down and clean-up?

We are. We arrive about ninety minutes before the start time so guests smell the chocolate as they enter. A professionally dressed attendant is there at all times, showing guests how to enjoy the fountain and keeping it pristine. Presentation and cleanliness matter to us; attendants replenish the accompaniments and keep the table looking its best.

09

What are the table and electrical requirements?

A 120 V grounded outlet within 10 feet of the fountain is necessary. A sturdy table with a wooden top only is required — not the flimsier plastic tops. It will not hold the weight of the fountain properly and then the chocolate won’t flow as beautifully. The table should be an 8-foot rectangular table. Two 8-foot rectangular tables are required when the 44-inch fountain is requested. The 19-inch fountain and the 34-inch fountain can be on one 8-foot rectangular table.

10

Who decorates the area around the fountain?

We do. For a wedding reception, baby shower, graduation, birthday or Halloween party, the fabric decorates the table. For weddings we use designer fabrics that match your color scheme.

11

What happens to the leftover chocolate?

Bring a few small plastic containers with tight-fitting lids and we will send the premium chocolate home with you. It keeps beautifully for about a year in the freezer. If you would rather not, it is disposed of. We never recycle chocolate from customer to customer.

12

What is required to reserve the fountain?

A $200 non-refundable deposit. All major credit cards are accepted; a link will be sent to you so that no credit card information is shared with us. A 3.3% surcharge is added to the quote if you decide to pay by credit card, and 6% Pennsylvania sales tax is also included. A personal or corporate check or cash is also OK. After the deposit we send two contracts by mail: sign both, keep one, return the other. The balance is due two weeks before the event.

One more question? Ask us directly.
