The important notice

Caterer, not us

Dipping items and plating for the Ranch Dressing Fountain are provided by your caterer. Chocolate Fountain Heaven supplies the fountain, the dressing and the attendant.

Ask your caterer to plan the fried and fresh vegetables, the pickles and the tenders — and to arrange the plating. We will keep the dressing moving and the table clean.

Good to know

The ranch fountain is indoors only, on a sturdy, level table, with a 120-volt grounded outlet within ten feet. Two and a half hours is included. Overtime is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant.
