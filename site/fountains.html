The attended fountain

34 and 44 inches

The 34-inch fountain is our full-service workhorse. The six-tier, 44-inch Majestic is the one that stops conversation.

  • Delivery, set-up, tear-down and clean-up
  • 2.5 hours of enjoyment
  • Sephra milk, dark or white chocolate
  • Dipping items, priced per person
  • An attendant for the whole event
  • Table decorated with fabric to match your color scheme
  • We no longer use the decorative ring at the base of the fountain because it creates poor air circulation.

Overtime beyond the contract is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant. Additional chocolate for larger guest counts is included in the quote.

Newest addition

The 44-inch Majestic

Six tiers, forty-four inches, brushed stainless steel and a WhisperQuiet motor. This is the largest Sephra fountain we offer, and the one that becomes the centerpiece of the room.

It carries the same standard of service as every attended fountain: delivery, set-up, an attendant for the whole event, tear-down and clean-up. It suits a party of one hundred or two thousand, and it is quoted for your date and guest count.

03

Gender reveals

White, then pink or blue

The fountain starts white. Nobody in the room knows yet.

The mom and dad-to-be are given several small vials, only one of which contains either pink or blue food coloring. When the correct vial is chosen, either pink or blue comes flowing down the fountain.

Tell us the color and we will keep it a secret until the moment.

What reserves a date
The deposit

A signed contract and a $200 non-refundable deposit reserve your date. A date is not held until the deposit arrives. Once the deposit is received, two contracts are mailed out for signatures — sign one, keep one, return the other. Cash, Venmo, personal check or card.

The balance

Due 15 days before the event.

The small print

Prices exclude 6% Pennsylvania sales tax. Reservations are first come, first served. Attended service starts at $500 for smaller events and varies with the details.
