02

The attended fountain

34 and 44 inches
Attended · full service

The 34-inch fountain is our full-service workhorse. The six-tier, 44-inch Majestic is the one that stops conversation.

  • Delivery, set-up, tear-down and clean-up
  • 2.5 hours of enjoyment
  • Sephra milk, dark or white chocolate
  • Dipping items, priced per person
  • An attendant — often two — for the whole event
  • Table decorated with fabric to match your color scheme (we no longer use the fountain base because of poor air circulation)

Overtime beyond the contract is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant. Additional chocolate for larger guest counts is included in the quote.

03

Gender reveals

White, then pink or blue

The fountain starts white. Nobody in the room knows yet.

White chocolate runs over the tiers while guests help themselves and the guessing carries on around the table. When you give us the word, pink or blue goes in and the answer comes pouring down the tiers in front of everyone. Guests see white right up to the moment you decide to tell them.

  • White chocolate fountain for the first part of the party
  • Pink or blue, added when you give the word
  • Two fountains, one pink and one blue, if both sides should pour
  • Delivery, set-up, an attendant and clean-up included

Tell us the colour and we will keep it to ourselves until the moment. Every reveal is quoted for your date and guest count.

Booking & payment

What reserves a date
The deposit

A signed contract and a $200 non-refundable deposit reserve your date. A date is not held until the deposit arrives. Cash, Venmo, personal check or card.

The balance

Due fifteen days before the event. A check in the mail holds a date for three business days only. Two contracts go out by mail after the deposit — sign one, keep one, return the other.

The small print

Prices exclude 6% Pennsylvania sales tax. Reservations are first come, first served. Attended service starts at $500 for smaller events and varies with the details.
