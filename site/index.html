02

The dippers

Dipping items are always included.

Fresh fruit and other delightful things to send through the chocolate. Because of liability and freshness, only Chocolate Fountain Heaven provides the dipping items — priced per person and tailored to your event.

  • Strawberries 
  • Pineapple 
  • Banana 
  • Apple 
  • Grapes 
  • Angel food cake 
  • Brownies 
  • Butter cookies 
  • Marshmallows 
  • Pretzel rods 
  • Rice-cereal squares 
  • Waffle pieces 

A sample selection — ask for the full list and a per-person quote. Avoid anything crumbly such as pound cake: it breaks apart and interrupts the flow. We recommend angel food cake for its sturdier texture.

03

How an evening runs

Set-up, tear-down and clean-up are ours.
i.

We arrive early

About ninety minutes before your start time, so guests meet the aroma of chocolate as they walk in.

ii.

We dress the table

Theme-based fabrics, or designer fabrics matched to your wedding colors. The table is part of the presentation.

iii.

We stay with it

A professionally dressed attendant keeps the fountain pristine and the accompaniments replenished. Two and a half hours is included.

iv.

We leave it spotless

Teardown and clean-up are included. Overtime is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant.

Room requirements

One 120-volt grounded outlet within ten feet, and a sturdy, level table. An unlevel table stops the chocolate from flowing. A table skirt from the venue completes the look.

Leftover chocolate

Bring a few small containers with tight lids and we will send the remaining premium chocolate home with you. It freezes beautifully for about a year. We never recycle chocolate between customers.

Indoors only

We do not recommend the fountain outdoors: the aroma attracts every heavenly creature in the garden, and the chocolate needs a stable temperature.

Questions

Before you book

The five we are asked most. The rest are on the FAQ page.

01

How long can we have the fountain?

Our package includes two and a half hours — usually the appetizer segment. Overtime is $50 per half hour, payable in cash to the attendant.

02

Do you provide the dipping items?

Yes. Because of liability and freshness, only Chocolate Fountain Heaven provides the dipping items. They are priced on a per-person basis and included in your quote.

03

Who handles set-up and clean-up?

We do. We arrive about ninety minutes early, and an attendant stays with the fountain throughout. Teardown and clean-up are included.

04

What do you need from the venue?

A 120-volt grounded outlet within ten feet, a sturdy level table, and a table skirt. Please confirm with your banquet manager before the day.

05

How do we reserve a date?

A signed contract and a $200 non-refundable deposit. The balance is due fifteen days before the event. Dates are first come, first served.
