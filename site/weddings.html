A note on timing

Most couples run the fountain during the appetiser hour. Our package includes two and a half hours, which is usually plenty. If you would rather keep it pouring through dinner and dancing, additional time is quoted at $35 per half hour.

We arrive ninety minutes before your start time, so the aroma meets guests as they walk in.
